About the Founder

Ravi Kale, a qualified civil engineer, founded Ravi Kale Builders in 2008 on a simple belief — that a building should be as honest as the people who make it. With over sixteen years in construction and civil contracting across Belgaum and North Karnataka, he has led the delivery of homes, commercial spaces, and institutional projects known for their engineering discipline and on-time handover.

His hands-on approach, attention to detail, and refusal to compromise on quality have earned the lasting trust of families and businesses across the region — making the firm one of the names Belgaum builds with.

About the Director

Sanjay Kale brings over fifteen years of experience in procurement, operations, and client relations to the firm. Having worked across banking, sales, and manufacturing before joining the business, he ensures every project runs smoothly — from the first cost estimate to the final handover.

He believes the true measure of the company's work is a client who feels genuinely at home in the space we build for them.
